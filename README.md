# Product-Vision-And-Strategy-With-Case-Studies
Things to keep in mind while writing a product vision &amp; strategy document. 

# Product Vision
The product vision document is one of the most important piece of document that a PM writes. This document must be articulated with absolute clarity, precision & must be shared with all the relevant stakeholders & peers in your company. Product vision document must be able to answer the following questions:

1. Who are your personas?
2. What are the goals of your personas?
3. What type solution/feature would help to achieve the personas goals?
4. What will be KPIs and product metrics which you would track?
5. What trade offs would you negotiate, if any?
6. What will be the future improvements?

# Product Strategy
The product strategy is more of a thought process that enables a PM to have a more strateguc approach to problem solving. A well defined product startegy must have the following attributes:

1. It must specifically define a focus area. A target market or persona. Before we serve everyone, we must serve someone and collect feedback.
2. It must be in tune with the business objective & startegy.
3. It must focus on solving a customer pain point/need/desire and not just copy what a competitor is doing.
4. It must be in tune with sales, operations, legal, marketing & other relevant teams in your company.
5. It must be effectively communicated with other relavnat teams and stakeholder. In product management, there is nothing such as "over communication". A well defined product startegy is meaningless if not communicated with the relevant stakeholders.
